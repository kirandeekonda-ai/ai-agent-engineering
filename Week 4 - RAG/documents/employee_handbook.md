# Acme Corp Employee Handbook

## Chapter 1: Leave Policy

### Annual Leave
All full-time employees are entitled to 20 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Leave must be requested at least 2 weeks in advance for periods exceeding 3 days.

### Sick Leave
Employees receive 10 days of sick leave annually. A medical certificate is required for absences exceeding 2 consecutive days. Unused sick leave does not carry over to the next year.

### Parental Leave
Primary caregivers are entitled to 16 weeks of paid parental leave. Secondary caregivers receive 4 weeks of paid leave. Leave must be taken within 12 months of the child's birth or adoption.

## Chapter 2: Remote Work Policy

### Eligibility
All employees who have completed their 3-month probation period are eligible for remote work. Department manager approval is required.

### Work Hours
Remote employees must be available during core hours (10 AM - 4 PM local time). Total working hours remain 40 per week.

### Equipment
The company provides a laptop and $500 stipend for home office setup. Employees are responsible for reliable internet connectivity.

## Chapter 3: Expense Policy

### Travel Expenses
Business travel must be pre-approved by the department head. Economy class flights are standard for trips under 6 hours. Meal allowance is $75 per day for domestic travel and $100 for international.

### Professional Development
Each employee has a $2,000 annual budget for courses, conferences, and certifications. Unused budget does not carry over.

### Equipment Purchases
Equipment over $200 requires manager approval. All company-purchased equipment remains company property.

## Chapter 4: Performance Reviews

### Review Cycle
Performance reviews occur bi-annually in June and December. Self-assessments are due 2 weeks before the review meeting.

### Ratings
Employees are rated on a 5-point scale:
1. Does Not Meet Expectations
2. Partially Meets Expectations
3. Meets Expectations
4. Exceeds Expectations
5. Significantly Exceeds Expectations

### Compensation Adjustments
Salary adjustments are determined annually based on performance ratings, market data, and company performance. The standard adjustment cycle is January 1st.
